# TIAMO background music

User-supplied T-ara TIAMO MP3, 203.52 seconds, 320 kbps, 44.1 kHz stereo. Copied as assets/tiamo.mp3 without transcoding.

The page attempts playback immediately at volume 0.18 and loops. Browser restrictions may require the first tap or keyboard interaction before audible playback starts. Page and modal both have synchronized pause/play controls. Manual pause persists during this visit and later flower taps do not restart playback.

Audio source resolves relative to music.js, so both project-root and garden/ entry points work. Real-device autoplay depends on the browser policy and device settings.
